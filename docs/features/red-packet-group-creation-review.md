# Red Packet and Group Creation Edge Case Review

**Status** In review. Resolve P0 decisions before engineering handoff.  
**Decision owners** Assign one named Product owner for Red Packet and one for Group Chat. Roles below are proposed reviewers.  
**Evidence** Repository review at `7442f04`. The linked specs contain historical browser QA. Every scenario below is untested in this review.

## Scope and current behavior

- **Red Packet** covers creation from Wallet, History or chat, PIN confirmation, sharing, claims, settlement and recovery. Lucky draw is the default, uses a budget without a recipient count and calculates the prize at claim time. Random and Equal split use a count, with a minimum of 1 MYRT and a maximum of 100 slots. All three modes currently charge zero fees. One packet budget is shared across every copy of its link, QR and chat card. [Product rules](red-packet-lucky-draw.md) · [Prototype store](../../prototype/features/red-packet/store.js)
- **Group Creation** covers the Group tab entry, eligibility, member selection, group details and permissions, creation, first chat, and initial invitations. Approved KYC users can create a group with only themselves. The name is required and capped at 40 characters. Description and photo are optional. [Group Chat PRD](PRD%203%202/PRD-Group-Chat.md) · [Group creation handlers](../../prototype/features/group-chat/groups.js) · [Active form and invite overrides](../../prototype/features/group-chat/messaging.js)

## Red Packet assessment and acceptance scenarios

P0 blocks handoff. P1 covers material recovery, scope or usability. The expected results are proposed where the product rules leave a decision open.

| ID | Priority | Scenario | Decision or expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RP-01 | P0 | Submit PIN twice, retry after timeout, switch devices after confirmation, or lose the response after debit | One send creates one durable packet and debits once. A retry returns its result. Define Pending, Confirmed and Failed states, including how History recovers the result after app restart. |
| RP-02 | P0 | Balance, KYC, wallet status, or limits change between form and PIN. Another transaction spends the balance first | Recheck at commit and preserve the draft on rejection. Agree frozen or restricted wallet behavior on send, claim and refund, and whether refunds restore monthly limit capacity. |
| RP-03 | P0 | Two accounts compete for the last slot, or one opens via chat and QR simultaneously. A claim races with cancellation or expiry | One payout per account. No payout after closure wins the race. Use server time for the exact expiry boundary. The amount paid plus the amount refunded reconciles with the original held amount once closed. |
| RP-04 | P0 | Claim or refund commits while its response or wallet-history update fails. Sender closes the app before expiry | Recover an authoritative receipt and balance. Settlement and retries work while all devices are offline. Preserve previous payouts when refunding a remainder. Define pending and failed statuses, notifications and escalation for unresolved transactions. |
| RP-05 | P0 | Chat stops accepting messages during PIN, or debit succeeds but card delivery fails | Current send flow can show a funded packet as ready to share if its original chat is unavailable. Decide whether to keep it funded with a clear “Created, delivery failed” state and retry or cancel actions. A delivery retry uses the same packet. [Send flow](../../prototype/features/red-packet/screens.js#L486) |
| RP-06 | P1 | Guest, KYC pending or rejected, inactive or disconnected wallet, PIN canceled or locked, top-up interrupted, or account changed during recovery | Define the next action for each state. Resume the exact draft or incoming token after successful recovery, then revalidate. Confirm that Back works without funding or claiming and that drafts are isolated by account. |
| RP-07 | P1 | Enter blank, negative, pasted or localized amounts, fractional count, 0, 1, 100 or 101 slots, or the exact balance and limits | Confirm amount precision, rounding and MYRT versus RM display. Check Random at count × 1 MYRT, Equal at amount × count, and Lucky at 1 MYRT and when the remaining funds fall below it. Show the payable total and actionable field errors. |
| RP-08 | P1 | Select custom expiry at its minimum, maximum, or out of range, cross time zones, or leave a never-expiring packet with zero claims | Specify when expiry starts and how it appears on the receipt. Agree the default expiry and what support can do about long-lived held funds. Show the correct remaining amount and refund timing for every mode. |
| RP-09 | P1 | Open as sender, repeat claimant or first-time recipient after a packet fills, expires or is canceled. Open a malformed, missing or stale token | A prior claimant can recover their receipt from History. Decide whether an ended card should also show the previous payout. Define states for invalid, canceled, expired, fully claimed and unavailable, and the visibility of claimant names and amounts. |
| RP-10 | P1 | Publish new rates during PIN, submit invalid or stale rates, claim with a budget below the highest prize, or tie for Luckiest | Version the rates at the agreed point and persist the exact version on the packet. Keep the minimum prize eligible, renormalize eligible weights, and apply the earlier-claim tie break. Decide who can publish, approve, audit and roll back rates and when users see the prize rules. |
| RP-11 | P1 | Leave during animation, reopen from History, or use VoiceOver, TalkBack or reduced motion | Show the same settled result without requiring animation. Announce progress and errors, avoid duplicate actions, and hide or disable “Say thanks” and “Send one back” where the relevant chat is unavailable. |

## Group Creation assessment and acceptance scenarios

| ID | Priority | Scenario | Decision or expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GC-01 | P0 | Create from the empty and populated Group tabs as a guest or with KYC unstarted, pending, rejected or revoked while the form is open | Apply the same eligibility policy at entry and commit. Specify the gate and whether sign-in or KYC approval resumes the picker or draft. Confirm any restricted-account policy. |
| GC-02 | P0 | One selected friend deactivates, blocks the creator, becomes ineligible, or drops out of the friends list before submission | Decide who can be added and whether direct additions require consent. Recommend showing affected names and asking the creator to review the remaining selection before creating. Define notifications, history visibility and handling for minors or blocked relationships if supported. |
| GC-03 | P0 | Double-tap Create, lose the response, crash and retry, or succeed at group creation while permissions, member additions or initial events fail | Create one group with one Owner and consistent settings. The Group and All tabs and reopened chat agree on the result. Define when the operation fails as a whole and which side effects can retry without duplicate notification or welcome events. |
| GC-04 | P0 | Create or join at capacity, select beyond capacity, or race a direct add against two invite joins for the final slot | Agree capacity, whether the Owner counts, and whether there are per-user creation or invitation limits. Use the same rules across selection, direct adds and invites. Provide a clear Full state and safe retry. |
| GC-05 | P0 | Open an invite after reset, rename, permissions change, removal, disband or at the same time as reset | Decide whether disabling sharing also invalidates existing invites, whether removed users may rejoin, whether the preview leaks member information, and how links behave after rename. Apply the same validity rules to links, QR and in-app cards. The prototype rotates the displayed token while the in-chat join handler routes by group ID, so reset enforcement needs end-to-end verification. [Invite link implementation](../../prototype/features/group-chat/messaging.js#L305) · [Join handler](../../prototype/features/group-chat/groups.js#L237) |
| GC-06 | P1 | Skip selection, search with no results, select the same friend across sections, remove and re-add someone, or reopen the picker | Owner-only creation remains available. Preserve a unique selection across search and Back, distinguish no friends from no matches, and reconcile the final member count. Confirm whether Recent chats and Friends remain required in the current version. |
| GC-07 | P1 | Enter whitespace-only text, identical group names, emoji and mixed scripts, or paste past the name and description limits | Require a trimmed name and agree how character limits are counted and shown. Decide duplicate-name and content moderation policies. Invite routing resolves stable group identity even when names collide. |
| GC-08 | P1 | Deny photo access, cancel crop, choose an unsupported or large image, or lose connectivity during upload | Keep the draft and prior photo. Offer retry or continue with a placeholder. Agree accepted types and sizes and verify the native picker and permission recovery on iOS and Android. The prototype uses preset gallery images. |
| GC-09 | P1 | Set permissions before Create, Back out of permissions, or leave the creation flow via system Back, gesture or app termination | Confirm defaults, save and discard behavior, and draft lifetime. Reopen the form with the agreed saved settings and check that the first member sees the same permissions after creation. |
| GC-10 | P1 | Open first invite signed out, on the wrong account, already a member, without the app, or after network loss | Define sign-in and KYC continuation, already-member routing, and whether deferred deep linking after install is in scope. If unavailable, provide a clear fallback. Verify share-sheet cancellation and copy or QR-save failures. |
| GC-11 | P1 | Owner leaves an empty new group, exits with admins or members present, or immediately disbands | Decide whether to block exit, transfer ownership, or close the group. ## Shared decision on group Red Packets

**P0 · Group audience and membership changes.** The group packet design describes group member counts and one draw per person. The prototype receive path checks sign-in, KYC, wallet access and token state, with no group membership check in that path. Decide whether group-scoped packets can be forwarded and claimed outside the group, whether new members are eligible, and what removal or departure does to a claim in progress. The proposed default is to restrict group-origin packets to current group members and preserve settled payouts and receipts. Agree how one-to-one and Wallet-created packets behave when shared to groups. Also decide whether disbanding the originating group leaves the packet active, closes and refunds it, or moves management to Wallet History. [Group packet product rules](red-packet-lucky-draw.md) · [Prototype receive state](../../prototype/features/red-packet/screens.js#L197)

**Acceptance scenario.** Create a group with the Owner and two friends. Send each packet mode from the group. Test one forwarded link with an outsider, one recipient added after send, one removed while the packet is open, one sender removed after payment, and one group disbanded with a remaining balance. Confirm the approved outcome across chat card, link and QR, including receipt access and the final balance. Repeat for a group with only its Owner and for a member whose send permission changes while confirming payment.

| Group packet audience and lifecycle | Red Packet Product owner, with Group Chat Product sign-off | Wallet and Chat Engineering, Design, QA | Approved eligibility matrix and verified cross-feature scenario. |

Run browser checks through the normal entry points for draft, navigation and rendered states. Run multi-account and failure scenarios against a server-backed environment. Use native iOS and Android builds to verify app links, system Back or gestures, photo and share permissions, accessibility, and interrupted-session recovery. Record platform, revision, preconditions and result for each scenario.

Proposed measures are group creation completion and first successful invite acceptance, plus packet send completion, claim completion and unresolved transaction counts. Establish baselines before assigning targets. For settlement QA, require zero duplicate debits, credits or refunds in the test scenarios.
