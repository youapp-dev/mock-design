# Logos

The YouApp mark (a leaf inside a ring) with the condensed YOUAPP wordmark, copied as vectors from the brand guidelines (Version 3.0, 2026). Never redraw, re-colour or re-space them.

- `youapp-horizontal-gradient`: the default lockup on white or light surfaces. The mark and wordmark carry the Primary gradient.
- `youapp-horizontal-gradient-mark-ink`: gradient mark with the wordmark in `brand-black`. Use it where the gradient wordmark would be too small to read.
- `youapp-horizontal-ink`: one colour, `brand-black` (#1A181B). For monochrome or print.
- `youapp-horizontal-on-dark`: gradient-filled mark with a white wordmark, for `brand-black` or dark photography.
- `youapp-horizontal-white`: all white (#FFFFFF), for the Primary gradient or dark imagery.
- `youapp-stacked-*`: the same five versions with the wordmark under the mark, for splash screens and square placements.
- `youapp-app-icon-circle` and `youapp-app-icon-rounded`: the white mark on a gradient circle or rounded square. Use them for app icons, avatars of the official account, and push-notification badges.

Keep clear space of at least the ring's radius on every side. The horizontal lockup should be at least 96px wide.
