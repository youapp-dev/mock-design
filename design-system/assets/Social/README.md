# Social

Third-party brand logos for social login and sharing, in their own colours: Facebook, Apple, Google, LinkedIn, Instagram, LINE, TikTok, WeChat and X. Show them only as `<img>` at 20–24px inside a Tertiary button or a share sheet. Never recolour or crop them.

`instagram.png` is a 192px raster because its layered gradient can't be stored as SVG here. Show it at 24px or smaller.
